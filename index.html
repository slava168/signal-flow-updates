Signal Flowv0.33.0

A cloud, AI-powered designer for your hi-fi audio signal chain. Snap a photo of your rack and let AI build the chain for you — or draw it by hand: drag the boxes, wire typed cables, add product photos. Then share it as a clean image, and let an AI consultant explain the flow, flag mismatches, and recommend upgrades. Your work is saved automatically.

Source→ DAC→ Preamp→ Power Amp→ Speakers

✨ Build with AI

Paste a forum post, a component list or notes — and drop in as many photos as you like. Paste-and-forget: Google Gemini reads text and images together for sharp device recognition, skips duplicate and irrelevant shots, merges repeat views of one device, and drops a single chain into a new flow or one you choose.

Visual scheme

Freely drag stage boxes on a zoomable canvas — arrange your system exactly the way it's wired.

Typed connections

Label each link: analog interconnect or speaker cable; digital USB, optical, coaxial (S/PDIF), AES/EBU, HDMI or I²S; wired network or Wi-Fi; power. One box can feed many.

35 stage types

Sources (streamer, tuner, CD/transport, tape, NAS), a full vinyl front-end (turntable, cartridge, step-up transformer, phono stage), DACs, room-correction/DSP, AV processors, pre/power/integrated amps, receivers, headphone amps, speakers, a center-channel speaker, subwoofers and headphones, network gear, power (conditioners, strips, PSUs, outlets) and support/isolation (platforms, feet, bases, stands).

Per-stage details

Tag each device's roles and I/O — streamer, Ethernet in, USB source, linear PSU — and add a product photo.

AI analysis

A senior-consultant AI (Google Gemini) reads your chain and writes a professional report: the verdict, the single weak link with the engineering reason, gain/impedance mismatches and redundant conversions, then prioritised, budget-aware upgrades — accurate by design, never inventing specs or prices.

One giant canvas

Every flow is a window on a single, spacious workspace — drag it anywhere by its handle, resize it from the corner, or fold it to its title bar to give another flow the room. Its stage canvas and AI consultant resize and move as one unit, and the workspace grows its height automatically so a flow dragged past the edge is never hidden.

Snap, fill & arrange

macOS-style window controls: snap a flow to the left, right, top or bottom half, fill the whole canvas, or auto-tile every flow into an even grid — one click, no dragging.

Scale to fit

Resize a flow window and the scheme fills the width at the largest readable size — no wasted side space. A tall chain scrolls; a wide one fills edge to edge. Prefer a fixed zoom? Drag the slider; hit Fit to snap back to auto. Drag a stage box's corner to resize it too.

Compact, high-density UI

Slim, crisp chrome — like a Mac's “More Space” display mode — keeps the controls out of the way and gives your signal chains far more room.

Reroute cables

Grab a cable and bend it anywhere — it stays pinned to both stages. Double-click to snap it back.

Flow view

A minimalist block-diagram schematic of your chain — plain boxes joined by thin arrowed connectors with cables named on each line, laid out just as you arranged them, endpoints lightly shaded like a classic signal-flow chart. Zoom it in and out to read every label. Copy it as clean text to paste straight into WhatsApp, a hi-fi forum, X, or email.

Screenshot the canvas

One click saves a crisp PNG of your canvas exactly as arranged — real boxes, wired cables and product photos, at full resolution.

Auto-save

Every box, cable and photo is saved on your device as you go — a refresh or an accidental reset won't lose your work.
