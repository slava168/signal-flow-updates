Signal Flowv0.14.0

A cloud, AI-powered designer for your hi-fi audio signal chain. Snap a photo of your rack and let AI build the chain for you — or draw it by hand: drag the boxes, wire typed cables, add product photos. Then share it as a clean image, and let an AI consultant explain the flow, flag mismatches, and recommend upgrades. Your work is saved automatically.

Source→ DAC→ Preamp→ Power Amp→ Speakers

✨ Build with AI

Paste a forum post, a component list or notes — and add photos of your rack and cabling. AI reads text and images together, reconciles them into one signal chain, and drops it into a new flow or one you choose.

Visual scheme

Freely drag stage boxes on a zoomable canvas — arrange your system exactly the way it's wired.

Typed cables

Draw connections and label each as interconnect, speaker, network, power, USB or optical. One box can feed many.

18 stage types

Sources, DACs, room-correction/DSP, pre/power & integrated amps, AV processors, speakers, plus network gear (modem, router, switch) and power (conditioners, strips, PSUs, outlets).

Per-stage details

Tag each device's roles and I/O — streamer, Ethernet in, USB source, linear PSU — and add a product photo.

AI analysis

An AI consultant spots the weak link, gain/impedance mismatches, and redundant conversions.

Compare systems

Build several signal flows side by side and analyze any selection to see which is strongest and why.

Resize everything

Drag the corner of any stage box to resize it, and drag the canvas corner to grow the whole workspace.

Reroute cables

Grab a cable and bend it anywhere — it stays pinned to both stages. Double-click to snap it back.

Share your flow

Copy your chain as a clean boxes-and-arrows diagram to paste straight into WhatsApp, a hi-fi forum, X, or email — no image needed.

Screenshot the canvas

One click saves a crisp PNG of your canvas exactly as arranged — real boxes, wired cables and product photos, at full resolution.

Auto-save

Every box, cable and photo is saved on your device as you go — a refresh or an accidental reset won't lose your work.
